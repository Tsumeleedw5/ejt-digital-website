THE STORY

EJT Digital was started by Edward J. Tsumele after a run of frustration: watching great local service businesses — cleaners, salons, security firms — lose deals to competitors with worse work but better front doors.

The insight was simple: most small businesses don't need "marketing." They need a system. Installed once, run forever — with a real operator on the other side of the WhatsApp when something needs changing.

Today, EJT Digital serves South African service businesses doing R10K – R100K per month, giving them the visual and operational standard the rest of the market is charging enterprise money for. Six seats onboarded per month, then we're full.

Every EJT client gets me directly — not an account manager, not a junior. When you WhatsApp EJT Digital, you're WhatsApping me.
